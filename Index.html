3.1.2 Concept & Motivation

The motivation behind designing a new tractor within Heracles AB stems from the strategic need to expand the company's product line with an innovative earthmoving machine for agriculture and construction sites.

Market research identified a gap for compact earthmoving machines inspired by American bulldozers. A central part of the concept is the integration of a swing tower similar to excavators, providing flexibility and precision without compromising quality.

3.6 Design for Manufacture and Assembly (DFMA) & FMEA

Assembly and manufacturing strategies are derived from FMEA (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis). Geometric changes in the CAD model are triggered based on FMEA impact regarding human errors, supported by DFA (Design for Assembly) and DFM (Design for Manufacturing).

CAD DESIGN / 03 (SOLIDWORKS)

Vehicle Assembly, Spring Mechanics & Gear Ratios

📥 Download Full Technical Report (PDF)
Figure 3.1: Vehicle assembly (FordonSolid1)[cite: 18].
Figure 3.2: Spring setup (FordonSolid2)[cite: 19].
Figure 3.3: Gear train layout (FordonSolid3).

1. Overview & Mass Specifications

Complete SolidWorks vehicle design featuring a total mass of 8.74163215 kg[cite: 18], integrating precise structural components, spring suspension mechanics, and symmetrical gear transmission systems.

2. Spring Mechanics & Calculations

Spring material conforms to standard SS 1770 with an ultimate tensile strength ($R_m$) of 1250 MPa.

2.4.1 Spring Deflection Calculation ($\Delta x = x_1 - x_0$)

$F_g = 265\text{ kg} \cdot 9.82\text{ m/s}^2 = 2602.3\text{ N}$

$k = 20000\text{ N/m}$

$(x_1 - x_0) = \frac{F_g}{k} = \frac{2602.3\text{ N}}{20000\text{ N/m}} = 0.130115\text{ m} = 13.0115\text{ cm} \approx 13\text{ cm}$

2.4.2 Energy Storage Calculation

$E = \frac{1}{2} \cdot k \cdot (x_1 - x_0)^2 = \frac{20000\text{ N/m} \cdot (0.130115\text{ m})^2}{2} = 169.2999132\text{ J} \approx 170\text{ J}$

3. Gear Train System Analysis (Figure ABCD)

The power transmission system consists of four primary gears (A, B, C, and D) structured symmetrically on both the left and right sides (excluding the right-side braking mechanism).

  • Gear A: 15 teeth
  • Gear B: 30 teeth
  • Gear C: 20 teeth
  • Gear D: 30 teeth

Gear Ratio Breakdown (Left & Right Sides):

$\frac{A}{B} = \frac{15}{30} = 0.5$

$\frac{B}{C} = \frac{30}{20} = 1.5$

$\frac{C}{D} = \frac{20}{30} = \frac{2}{3}$

Total Ratio ($R_{\text{total}}$):

$R_{\text{total}} = \left(\frac{A}{B}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{B}{C}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{C}{D}\right) = 0.5 \cdot 1.5 \cdot \frac{2}{3} = 0.5$

Conclusion: Both sides yield an identical total transmission ratio of 0.5, ensuring perfectly balanced mechanical performance.
