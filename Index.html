PROJECT / 02

BMS-Projekt 1

GitHub Repository: https://github.com/wowercan1337-jpg/bms-projekt1 ↗

Introduction

Battery Management Services (Part 1): A smart client designed to interact with a simulated EV charger via JSON HTTP to optimize household charging.

Core Requirements & Simulation

Reads hourly electricity prices (Price Area 3, Stockholm) alongside 24-hour household baseload profiles. Converts EV battery energy (kWh) to percentage and manages charging cycles from 20% to 80% (default capacity 60 kWh).

Operational Modes

1. Lowest Household Consumption: Keeps total household + charger load $\le 11\text{ kW}$.

2. Lowest Electricity Price: Schedules charging during cheap hours while respecting the $11\text{ kW}$ threshold.

Simulator Endpoints & Workflow

Utilizes endpoints such as GET /info, GET /baseload, GET /priceperhour, and POST/GET /charge to poll status, compute optimal hours, and output comprehensive timelines.

Randomized Physics Formulas

Friction Force: $F_f = \mu \cdot F_g \cdot \cos(\alpha)$
